Jackbox on Apple TV, and the alternatives

Updated 27 November 2026

We make Joypad Party, one of the alternatives below. Everything about Jackbox comes from Jackbox's own pages and its App Store listings, checked that day. We're not affiliated with Jackbox Games; Jackbox, Quiplash, Fibbage and its other game names belong to it.

On Apple TV · iPad · iPhone. On the Apple TV, search "Joypad Party" in its App Store.

Yes, you can play Jackbox on Apple TV: Jackbox sells its party packs and several single games on the App Store for Apple TV. You start the game on the TV, everyone joins by opening jackbox.tv in their phone's browser and typing the room code, and only the host needs to own the game.

How to play Jackbox on Apple TV

  1. Get it on the TV. On the Apple TV, open the App Store, search for Jackbox and get a Party Pack (or a single game, such as Quiplash or Fibbage XL).
  2. Open it and pick a game from the pack's menu with the Siri Remote.
  3. Join on your phones. Everyone opens jackbox.tv in their phone's browser and types the room code on the TV. Nobody installs an app.
  4. Start when everyone's in. Only the host owns the game.

How Jackbox works on Apple TV, step by step

  1. Get a pack on the Apple TV. Open the App Store on the Apple TV and search for Jackbox. The packs and the single games are listed separately; prices are on each listing.
  2. Open it and pick a game from the pack's menu.
  3. The TV shows a room code. Each player opens jackbox.tv in their phone's browser, types the code and a name. Nobody installs an app, and Jackbox's listings say phones, tablets and computers all work as controllers, so the friend with an Android phone plays too.
  4. Start when everyone's in. In many games, people beyond the player limit can join as the audience and still vote. The Jackbox Party Pack 8, for example, lists up to 10 players and up to 10,000 audience members.

The rule that matters most at a party is on Jackbox's own how-to page: "Only one person in your group needs to own a Jackbox product in order to host the game."

Which pack to start with

Jackbox doesn't rank its packs, and neither will we. What the listings tell you:

  • You want the best-known games in one box: The Jackbox Party Starter collects Quiplash 3, Trivia Murder Party 2 and Tee K.O., with voices and subtitles in five more languages.
  • You have more than eight people: look for games that list up to 10 players, such as Drawful Animate, Job Job and The Poll Mine in Party Pack 8 or Quixort in Party Pack 9, or Bracketeering (3–16) in Party Pack 4. Everyone else joins as the audience.
  • Not everyone reads English: Party Pack 10, The Jackbox Party Starter and The Jackbox Survey Scramble list other languages; Packs 1 to 8 say English only.
  • Kids are playing: Jackbox says many recent titles have a family-friendly setting. In Party Pack 10 it's under Settings › Content Control, next to the language.

Common Apple TV questions

The Siri Remote won't move through the pack's menu. Jackbox's help centre has an article for exactly this: I'm having trouble navigating a game with my Apple TV remote.

A phone can't get into the room. See Jackbox's I'm having trouble connecting my device to the game.

The game is in another language. In The Jackbox Party Pack 10, language is a setting: press Settings on the pack's menu screen and look under Content Control, which lists English, Castilian and Latin American Spanish, Italian, German, French and Brazilian Portuguese (Jackbox's post of 5 December 2023). For other packs, Jackbox's help centre is the place to ask.

Alternatives on Apple TV that also use phones

All of these were checked on the US App Store on 23 November 2026.

Joypad Party (ours)
One app with 13 games for 1 to 20 players: a quiz show, two drawing games, an imposter word game, bracket votes, a falling-block battle, a split-screen racer and shooter, a brawler, two co-op games, a minigame party and a dress-up runway. Each player uses the Joypad Party app on their iPhone or iPad; Android phones can't join yet. Without Joypad Pro, an optional subscription bought in the app on iPhone or iPad, up to 2 players can play and there's a daily limit on games; Pro unlocks every game and any number of players for the whole room. Hexcast and Showquiz also take the Siri Remote, tested in Apple's tvOS Simulator so far. No account and no ads; rated 13+ because of the shooter.

Netflix's TV games
Included with a Netflix membership. Each player uses a phone or tablet with the Netflix Game Controller app. Netflix's own list of TV party games (Tudum, 28 July 2026) includes The Jackbox Party Essentials, alongside Boggle Party and Pictionary: Game Night. Netflix's help pages say the games run on "many recent model TVs and TV streaming devices" and don't name Apple TV, so open Netflix on yours and look for a Games option first.

SketchParty TV
A team drawing game: one person draws on an iPad or iPhone, the drawing shows on the TV, and their team guesses. Up to 8 players a team, rated 4+.

Buzzin, poyo and Screens
Phone-controller party platforms whose App Store apps are listed for iPhone (and iPad), not Apple TV. Their listings describe casting the game to the TV or opening it in a TV's or computer's browser, so check how that works with your screen before the party.

When Jackbox is the better choice, and when it isn't

These are opinions, from a competitor; weigh them that way.

Jackbox suits a room of grown-ups who like writing jokes and bluffing, where the funniest answer wins. Its browser join is the simplest option when the group mixes iPhones and Android phones, and the audience mode is good when twenty people turn up for a game that seats eight.

An alternative may fit better when everyone carries an iPhone and the evening wants more than writing games, such as a race or a brawl; when young kids who type slowly are playing; or when the group would rather point at a picture than type a punchline. That's the gap Joypad Party's games aim at: Scribblegram, for one, lets players write in any language. For a room that likes drawing, Drawshow puts one player's phone drawing live on the TV while everyone guesses; for bluffing without the typing, Snowbluff hands everyone the same secret word except one guest; and Pickoff lets up to 20 people vote picture match-ups.

Questions

Can you play Jackbox on Apple TV?

Yes. Jackbox sells its party packs and several single games on the App Store for Apple TV. Players join by opening jackbox.tv in their phone's browser and typing the room code, and only the host needs to own the game, according to Jackbox's own how-to page.

Do players need to buy Jackbox?

No. Jackbox says only one person in your group needs to own a Jackbox product to host the game. Everyone else joins at jackbox.tv in a web browser, with no app to install.

What's like Jackbox on Apple TV?

Other party games that run on Apple TV and use phones as controllers include Joypad Party (an app on each iPhone or iPad, 13 games for 1 to 20 players) and Netflix's TV games (with a Netflix membership, if your Apple TV shows a Games option). SketchParty TV is a team drawing game played from one iPad or iPhone.

Can you get Jackbox on Apple TV?

Yes. On the Apple TV, open the App Store and search for Jackbox: every Jackbox Party Pack from the first to Party Pack 12 is there, along with The Jackbox Party Starter, The Jackbox Survey Scramble, Fakin' It XL and the single games Quiplash, Fibbage XL and Drawful 2 (checked on the US App Store on 27 November 2026). Prices are on each listing.

How do you play Jackbox on Apple TV?

Open the pack on the Apple TV and pick a game. The TV shows a room code; each player opens jackbox.tv in their phone's browser, types the code and a name, and plays on the phone. When everyone's in, start the game. People beyond the player limit can join as the audience in many games.

Is there a Jackbox TV app for Apple TV?

Not under that name. jackbox.tv is the website the players open on their phones to join. What goes on the Apple TV is a Jackbox Party Pack or a single Jackbox game from the App Store, and that's the only thing anyone has to buy.

Which Jackbox Party Pack is on Apple TV?

All of them: The Jackbox Party Pack and Party Packs 2 to 12 each list Apple TV among their devices on the US App Store, as do The Jackbox Party Starter, The Jackbox Survey Scramble and The Jackbox Naughty Pack (rated 16+). The table on this page lists the games inside each one.

Can you play Quiplash on Apple TV?

Yes. Quiplash has its own Apple TV listing, last updated in 2016, and newer versions come inside packs: Quiplash XL in Party Pack 2, Quiplash 2 in Party Pack 3, and Quiplash 3 in Party Pack 7 and The Jackbox Party Starter. Each takes 3 to 8 players.

More: How to play Quiplash and Fibbage on Apple TV

Can you play Fibbage on Apple TV?

Yes. Fibbage XL is sold on its own for Apple TV and is also in The Jackbox Party Pack; Fibbage 2 is in Party Pack 2, Fibbage 3 in Party Pack 4 and Fibbage 4 in Party Pack 9. Each takes 2 to 8 players, who type their lies on their phones.

Can you play Jackbox with just the Siri Remote?

No. The Siri Remote gets you through the pack's menus, but the players play on their own phones, tablets or computers at jackbox.tv. Jackbox's listings call those devices the controllers.

Is SketchParty TV on Apple TV?

Yes. SketchParty TV's App Store listing names Apple TV, iPhone and iPad. It's a team drawing game: one person draws on an iPad or iPhone, the drawing shows on the TV, and their team guesses. Up to 8 players a team, rated 4+.
