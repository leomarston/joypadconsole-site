The best party games for Apple TV (2026)

Updated 23 November 2026

We make Joypad Party, one of the games below. We've kept our entry as factual as the others, included the games we compete with, and checked every listing on the US App Store on the same day.

The best party game for your Apple TV depends on who's coming. Jackbox is the pick for grown-ups who like writing jokes, SketchParty TV for a team drawing game around one iPad, and Joypad Party for a group where everyone has an iPhone and wants a mix of quiz, drawing, Werewolf and racing. Below: how each one works and how many people it takes. For prices, see each game's App Store page.

How we picked

Every game here had to:

  • run on the Apple TV itself, from the App Store on the TV (not just mirrored from a phone);
  • let several people play at once in the same room;
  • be live on the US App Store on 23 November 2026.

For each one we noted how players join (phone app, web browser, a shared iPad, the Siri Remote), how many can play, the App Store age rating and the languages. We don't rank anyone "number one". We sort by the kind of evening you're planning.

Best for grown-ups who like writing jokes: Jackbox

If your group's idea of a good night is typing the funniest answer to a prompt and voting on it, Jackbox is the reference. Its games are built around writing and bluffing: Quiplash (answer silly prompts; answers go head to head), Fibbage (type a convincing lie to a real trivia question), Drawful (draw a weird prompt on your phone; the others guess).

How it works on Apple TV. You buy a pack or a single game on the Apple TV's App Store. Players open jackbox.tv in their phone's browser and type the room code on the TV. Only the host needs to own the game, and because players join in a browser, iPhones, Android phones and tablets all work.

What to know.

  • The Jackbox Party Pack 8 runs on Apple TV and iPad, takes up to 10 players plus an audience, and is in English only.
  • Quiplash, Fibbage XL and Drawful 2 are also sold on their own; their App Store listings were last updated in 2016.
  • Jackbox says many of its recent titles have a family-friendly setting in the lobby that filters out suggestive prompts, and describes its games as "rated T for Teen". The App Store rates the packs 12+.
  • The audience feature is useful with a crowd: people beyond the player limit can join as the audience from their own devices and still affect the game.

Best team drawing game: SketchParty TV

SketchParty TV is a Pictionary-style team game. One person draws on an iPad (or iPhone), the drawing shows on the TV, and their team guesses. It has 4.7 stars from 1,446 ratings on the US App Store.

What to know. The App Store rates it 4+, and its listing says it's for ages 8 and up, with up to 8 players per team. It needs one iPad or iPhone for drawing rather than a phone per player, which makes it easy to start but means only the drawer touches a device. Its listing mentions AirPlay Mirroring.

If you'd rather everyone draw at once: Joypad Party has two phone-per-player drawing games. In Drawshow, one player draws on their phone while it appears live on the TV and everyone types guesses (3–16 players, English words). In Scribblegram, every player writes, draws and describes at the same time, and the TV plays the chains back at the end (3–20).

Best trivia night: depends on the room

  • For a straight quiz with up to 16 people: Showquiz in Joypad Party. Questions on the TV, everyone answers A to D on their own phone within 20 seconds, scoreboard after each question. Honest limit: the questions are in English only.
  • For trivia where bluffing matters more than knowing: Jackbox's Fibbage. You invent fake answers to real questions and score when others fall for them.
  • For one to four people with only the Siri Remote: Family Quiz Party plays on the remote, with multiplayer on the same screen as a premium unlock, according to its listing. No ratings yet on the US store.

Best for a big group (10 to 20 people)

  • Joypad Party takes 20 players in three games: Moonhowl (Werewolf narrated by the TV, 5–20), Scribblegram (3–20) and Pickoff (bracket votes and Would You Rather, 1–20). Every player needs an iPhone or iPad with the app.
  • Jackbox Party Pack 8 takes up to 10 players and lets everyone else join as the audience.

Which is better depends on your phones. If most of your guests carry iPhones, Joypad Party gets all 20 into the game itself. If it's a mix of iPhone and Android, Jackbox's browser join is simpler.

Best if everyone has an iPhone: Joypad Party

Full disclosure: this is ours. Joypad Party is one app with 14 games, where each player uses the Joypad Party app on their iPhone as the controller, and the phone's buttons change with the game.

What's in it. A quiz show, two drawing games, Werewolf with a narrator, an imposter word game, bracket votes, a falling-block battle, a split-screen racer and shooter, a brawler, two co-op games, a minigame party and a dress-up runway. Player counts run from 1–2 (the racer, the puzzle battle) to 5–20 (Werewolf).

What to know.

  • No ads and no account (checked 23 November 2026).
  • Phones and the TV talk over your home Wi-Fi; no internet connection is needed to play.
  • Six of the games run on every Apple TV that runs the app. Eight need an Apple TV 4K (2nd generation or later), and six of those also need tvOS 26.
  • Hexcast and Showquiz can be played with the Siri Remote; the rest need a phone per player.
  • Android phones can't join yet.
  • The App Store rates it 12+; one of the 14 games is a shooter.
  • It's new: no App Store ratings yet.

Best for two players

Most of the games above are built for groups. For an evening for two, Joypad Party has Hexcast, a falling-block battle that runs on any Apple TV that runs the app and also plays with the Siri Remote, and Latenight Streets, a split-screen racer. Apple Arcade has games with local multiplayer too. Our full list is in 2-player games on Apple TV.

Best with a Netflix plan: Netflix's TV games, if your Apple TV has them

Netflix makes party games for the TV, included with a membership. Its Tudum article of 28 July 2026 names Boggle Party, LEGO Party!, Pictionary: Game Night, Party Crashers: Fool Your Friends, Overcooked! All You Can Eat, Netflix Minigolf, The Jackbox Party Essentials and Tetris Time Warp. Each player uses their phone or tablet as the controller, with the Netflix Game Controller app on iPhone.

The catch for this page: Netflix's help pages say the games run on "many recent model TVs and TV streaming devices" and don't name Apple TV. To check yours, open Netflix on the Apple TV and look for a Games option at the top of the screen. If it's there, those games come with your membership.

What you need for a phone-controller party game

  • The same Wi-Fi for the TV and the phones, for app-based games like Joypad Party. Guest networks and some routers keep devices apart.
  • A phone per player, with the game's app (Joypad Party, Netflix Game Controller) or a web browser (Jackbox).
  • Somewhere everyone can read the TV. Most of the game happens on the big screen.
  • Ten minutes before guests arrive to install, open the game once and check that a phone connects. It's quicker than sorting it out in front of a full room. Here's how phone controllers work on Apple TV.

What we left out, and why

  • Buzzin, poyo and AirConsole. Their App Store apps are listed for iPhone and iPad, not Apple TV (checked 23 November 2026).
  • Charades and Chill and Square Gaming: both run on Apple TV, but their listings say very little and neither has ratings, so we couldn't judge them. Square Gaming was last updated in January 2024, Charades and Chill in December 2022.
  • Games you mirror from a phone with AirPlay. They can be fun, but the game runs on the phone, not the Apple TV.

Questions

Does Apple TV have party games?

Yes. The App Store on Apple TV has party games where several people play at once, usually with their phones as the controllers: Jackbox's party packs, SketchParty TV and Joypad Party, among others.

What's the best party game for Apple TV?

It depends on the group. Jackbox suits grown-ups who like writing jokes, SketchParty TV suits a room that wants a team drawing game, and Joypad Party suits a group where everyone has an iPhone and wants a mix of quiz, drawing, Werewolf and racing.

Can you play Jackbox on Apple TV?

Yes. Jackbox sells several of its games on the App Store for Apple TV. Players join by opening jackbox.tv in their phone's browser and typing the room code, and only the host needs to own the game.

Can you play Netflix's party games on Apple TV?

Netflix's help pages say its TV games run on many recent TVs and streaming devices, and don't name Apple TV. The quickest check is to open Netflix on your Apple TV and look for a Games option at the top.
