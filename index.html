Joypad Party is an app for Apple TV and iPad that turns the screen into a party game console. Everyone in the room plays at once on their own iPhone, which shows buttons made for each game: answer buttons in the quiz, a drawing canvas in the drawing games, a steering pad in the racer.

How it works

  1. 01

    Open Joypad Party on the Apple TV and on your iPhone.

  2. 02

    On the same Wi-Fi, the phone finds the TV by itself and joins within a few seconds. The first phone to join picks the games.

  3. 03

    Friends join the same way. Anyone without the app scans the QR code on the TV, which opens the App Store page.

If a phone can't find the TV, the TV shows a 6-digit code to type in. Everything else, including what to check when it won't connect, is in How it works.

What you need

A screenAn Apple TV on tvOS 17 or later, or an iPad on iPadOS 17 or later.
ControllersAn iPhone per player with Joypad Party (iOS 17 or later). An iPad can be a controller too.
The same Wi-FiFor the TV and the phones. No internet connection is needed to play.

Five games run on every Apple TV that runs the app: Hexcast, Showquiz, Drawshow, Pickoff and Scribblegram. The other eight need an Apple TV 4K (2nd generation or later), and some of them also need tvOS 26. Here's which Apple TV runs which game.

Showquiz, Drawshow and Pickoff are in English. The rest of the app comes in 13 languages.

No phone? Use the Siri Remote

Hexcast and Showquiz can also be played with the Siri Remote, so someone without an iPhone can still take a seat. The other games need a phone for each player. We've tested remote play in Apple's tvOS Simulator; a test on a real Apple TV is next. More on that in Apple TV games without a controller.
