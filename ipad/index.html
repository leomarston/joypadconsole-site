Your iPad is the console

Checked 23 November 2026 against Joypad Party 1.2

Put an iPad on a stand, open Joypad Party and choose Screen: the iPad becomes the console, and every iPhone on the same Wi-Fi joins it as a controller. It works the same way as on an Apple TV, from the same download, so you can play at a friend's place, on holiday, or in a room without a TV.

Set it up

  1. Install Joypad Party on the iPad and on each iPhone.
  2. On the iPad, choose Screen. The first time you open the app, the iPad shows "This iPad is the screen" with a choice: use this iPad as a Screen or a Controller.
  3. Turn the iPad sideways and stand it up where everyone can see it. The games are laid out for a wide screen; held upright, the picture gets black bars.
  4. Open the app on each iPhone, on the same Wi-Fi. The phones find the iPad by themselves. The first time, each iPhone asks to find devices on your local network: tap Allow.
  5. If a phone can't find the iPad, type the code shown on the iPad under "Can't connect?".

The first phone to join is the host and picks the games, just as with an Apple TV.

Switching between screen and controller

  • From screen to controller: the iPad's connect page has "Use this iPad as a controller instead".
  • From controller to screen: in the controller's menu, choose "Use this iPad as the screen".

An iPad as a controller is useful when someone doesn't have an iPhone: it joins an Apple TV (or another iPad) exactly like a phone.

Which iPads run which games

The app needs iPadOS 17 or later. After that it depends on the chip and the software:

GamesiPad needed
Hexcast, Showquiz, Drawshow, Pickoff, ScribblegramAny iPad on iPadOS 17 or later
Latenight Streets, TumbledownAn iPad with the A12 Bionic chip or later
Snowbluff, Lamplight Canteen, Talkdown, Boardwalk Bits, Haute CoopA12 Bionic or later, on iPadOS 18 or later
Final WatchA12 Bionic or later, on iPadOS 26 or later; downloads extra content the first time

The app only lists the games your iPad can run. If a game is missing, that's the reason.

Good to know

  • Local Network on the iPad, too. If Local Network is off for Joypad Party on the iPad, phones can't find it by themselves, but they can still join with the code. You can turn it on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.
  • More than one screen at home? If an Apple TV and an iPad are both open on the same Wi-Fi, a phone goes back to the one it used last, or asks you to pick.
  • A bigger iPad works better for a crowd. Scribblegram and Pickoff take up to 20 players, but 20 people can't all read an 11-inch screen. For a big group, an Apple TV is the better screen. See the Apple TV page.
  • Good first games on an iPad: Showquiz for the whole table and Scribblegram for a drawing round.

Questions

Can I use my iPhone as a game controller for my iPad?

In games built for it, yes. With Joypad Party, choose Screen on the iPad, then open the app on each iPhone on the same Wi-Fi. The phones find the iPad by themselves and become the controllers.

Can an iPad be a controller instead?

Yes. Choose Controller when the app asks, and the iPad joins an Apple TV or another iPad like a phone does. It's handy for a player who doesn't have an iPhone.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not to play. The iPad and the iPhones only need to be on the same Wi-Fi. You need the internet to download the app, and Final Watch downloads extra content the first time you open it.
